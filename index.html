FAQ

Questions owners ask us

Will customers know they're talking to an AI?

Yes. The agent introduces itself as your AI assistant. It's the honest approach, it's required in many places, and in practice callers mostly care that someone picked up and booked them in.

Does it work with my booking system?

We can connect it to Google Calendar and to many salon and clinic booking systems. Tell us which one you use and we'll confirm before you commit to anything.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. Most businesses keep their number and forward calls to the agent, either all calls or only the ones nobody answers.

What languages can it speak?

The agent can work in many languages, including English, Urdu, Hindi, Arabic and Spanish. We'll test your language in the demo before you go live.

What happens if it can't answer something?

It takes a message and sends it to you, or transfers the caller to your number. It never makes things up about your prices or policies.

How long does setup take?

A demo with your business details takes a day. Going live depends on your phone and booking setup, usually within a week.
